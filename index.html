📅 10-Week Study Plan

  1. Week 1 — Network Fundamentals, OSI & TCP/IP
  2. Week 2 — IPv4, Subnetting & IPv6
  3. Week 3 — Ethernet, Switching, VLANs & Trunks
  4. Week 4 — STP, EtherChannel & Inter-VLAN Routing
  5. Week 5 — Routing, Static Routes & OSPF
  6. Week 6 — DHCP, DNS, NAT, NTP & SSH
  7. Week 7 — Security Fundamentals, ACLs & Port Security
  8. Week 8 — Wireless, Automation & Programmability
  9. Week 9 — Packet Tracer Labs & Practice Questions
  10. Week 10 — Mock Exams & Review Weak Areas
Best method: Watch → Take notes → Build the lab → Troubleshoot → Quiz → Repeat.
